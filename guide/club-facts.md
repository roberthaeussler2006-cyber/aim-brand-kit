## What we do

Workshops, speaker events, panel talks, fireside chats, coaching, company visits, roundtables,
and the **Learning Journey**: a course of practical AI sessions for first-year students without a
finance or tech background.

We are not a coding club. Focus: how AI is used in consulting, banking, finance, insurance and
corporate functions.
